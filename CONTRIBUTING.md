# Contributing

The lab is designed around a consistent learner experience.

## Before adding a notebook

Ask:

1. What engineering problem does this teach?
2. What mental model should the learner leave with?
3. What should they predict before execution?
4. What can they measure?
5. What can we deliberately break?
6. How will they diagnose the failure?
7. What changes in production?
8. Where does this concept connect to previous/future modules?
9. What sketch note communicates the concept visually?
10. What independent challenge proves understanding?

## Definition of done

A notebook is ready when:

- it runs from a fresh Colab runtime;
- dependencies are explicit;
- the sketch note is present at the beginning;
- learning objectives are observable;
- at least one prediction checkpoint exists;
- at least one measurement exists where measurement is meaningful;
- at least one controlled failure exists where failure is meaningful;
- the learner diagnoses the failure rather than being shown only the answer;
- production implications are explicit;
- the README and manifest are updated;
- notebook links and Colab links are valid;
- the notebook is included in the current handoff/roadmap state.
