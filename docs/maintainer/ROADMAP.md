## Next quality work

1. **Deepen 25–30** with more real Spark/streaming/lakehouse observability while preserving Colab-first operation.

## Deliberately not planned yet

Do not add another broad topic phase until the existing labs demonstrate the intended depth consistently. The next meaningful improvement is authenticity and evidence, not more syllabus breadth.

## v1.5 direction

The next evolution keeps the 00–54 technical spine but makes agent-native engineering a horizontal curriculum layer: specification, context engineering, delegation, inspection, verification, authorization, human escalation and operational defense. The goal is not more AI-themed notebooks; it is a changed learner role across existing systems.
