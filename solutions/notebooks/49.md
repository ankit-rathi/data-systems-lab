# Reference solution — Notebook 49

## What the exercise is testing

Turn business requirements into measurable architecture constraints.

## Reference approach

Requirements should become measurable workload, latency/freshness, reliability, security and cost constraints. A strong architecture proposal names unknowns and defines verification evidence before implementation.

## What a strong submission should contain

- **Prediction:** what you expected before running the experiment.
- **Evidence:** the measurement, output, query result or trace that supports your conclusion.
- **Failure/edge case:** one case that could have disproved your explanation.
- **Diagnosis:** mechanism → evidence → conclusion.
- **Production boundary:** what this notebook proves, what it does not prove, and what you would verify in a real system.

## Compare, don't copy

Your implementation can differ from this reference. The important question is whether your answer is **correct, reproducible, evidenced and defensible** under the exercise constraints.
