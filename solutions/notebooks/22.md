## What the exercise is testing

Choose a read-after-write policy under replica lag.

## Reference approach

The trade-off is consistency/latency/availability. Leader reads give strong freshness but can add latency; sticky/session-aware reads preserve read-after-write for a client; quorum/wait-for-catch-up add cost/latency. The policy should be tied to business tolerance for stale data.
