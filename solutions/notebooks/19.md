## What the exercise is testing

See when compaction helps and when it becomes the bottleneck.

## Reference approach

Tombstones/overwrites create extra reads until compaction collapses obsolete state. Compaction reduces read amplification but consumes write/IO resources and can become a bottleneck under sustained churn.
