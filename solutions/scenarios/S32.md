# Reference solution — S32

## Before you compare

Finish the mission first. The reference below is a **defensible answer pattern**, not the only acceptable implementation.

## Reference diagnosis / solution

Incident recovery is complete only when service and important side effects/data are verified. Contain first, roll back/replay deliberately, and capture evidence that correctness and safety were restored.

## What the mission was asking you to prove

A new AI release increased error rate and duplicate support actions. Some failures are visible in system metrics; others appear only as business-side effects. You are the incident commander, not the person who originally wrote the change. Build a timeline, identify the blast radius, choose containment, test rollback, and verify both technical recovery and recovery of business-side effects.

## Compare your evidence

A strong submission should contain:

- the business symptom and impact;
- at least two plausible hypotheses and the evidence that separated them;
- the smallest justified implementation/configuration change;
- a regression or acceptance test;
- before/after measurements where applicable;
- a short technical explanation and a short business explanation;
- the operational implication: monitoring, prevention, recovery, security or cost as relevant;
- if an agent was used, the work contract, permissions, independent verification, challenge attempted and human decision.

## Definition of done

1. Build a timestamped incident timeline. 2. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. 3. Define blast radius and containment criteria. 4. Execute or simulate rollback with evidence. 5. Verify business-side-effect recovery separately from software recovery. 6. Produce follow-up actions with owners, evidence and regression tests.

## If your answer differs

That is acceptable when your evidence supports it and your solution respects the ticket constraints. Compare the **reasoning and evidence**, not just the final code or wording.
