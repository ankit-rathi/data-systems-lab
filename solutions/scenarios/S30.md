# Reference solution — S30

## Before you compare

Finish the mission first. The reference below is a **defensible answer pattern**, not the only acceptable implementation.

## Reference diagnosis / solution

Apply least privilege: the AI worker should receive only the data/tools/actions required for the task. Denied actions, actor identity, policy decision and audit evidence must be explicit.

## What the mission was asking you to prove

An AI workflow needs access to customer and payment-adjacent information to resolve support cases. The proposed workflow is useful, but its current tool/data boundary is wider than the business need. Determine the minimum authority the workflow requires, which data should remain inaccessible, how identity and authorization should be enforced, and what evidence must be logged for every sensitive action.

## Compare your evidence

A strong submission should contain:

- the business symptom and impact;
- at least two plausible hypotheses and the evidence that separated them;
- the smallest justified implementation/configuration change;
- a regression or acceptance test;
- before/after measurements where applicable;
- a short technical explanation and a short business explanation;
- the operational implication: monitoring, prevention, recovery, security or cost as relevant;
- if an agent was used, the work contract, permissions, independent verification, challenge attempted and human decision.

## Definition of done

1. Identify assets, actors, tools and trust boundaries. 2. Define least-privilege permissions for the workflow. 3. Separate read, write and irreversible actions. 4. Specify approval and audit requirements. 5. Add a misuse/negative test for an unauthorized request. 6. State the residual risk and escalation path.

## If your answer differs

That is acceptable when your evidence supports it and your solution respects the ticket constraints. Compare the **reasoning and evidence**, not just the final code or wording.
