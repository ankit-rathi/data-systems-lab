# Reference solution — S28

## Before you compare

Finish the mission first. The reference below is a **defensible answer pattern**, not the only acceptable implementation.

## Reference diagnosis / solution

Translate business requirements into measurable constraints, compare at least two architectures, expose unknowns, and define verification/stop conditions. The reference answer should reject an option when it violates a hard requirement even if it is technically elegant.

## What the mission was asking you to prove

Checkout analytics and AI support are both growing, but they have different latency, freshness, reliability and data-access requirements. The platform team wants one architecture proposal before the next release. Your job is not to draw a diagram first. Convert the business request into measurable requirements, identify incompatible assumptions, compare at least two architecture options, and record which evidence would change your decision.

## Compare your evidence

A strong submission should contain:

- the business symptom and impact;
- at least two plausible hypotheses and the evidence that separated them;
- the smallest justified implementation/configuration change;
- a regression or acceptance test;
- before/after measurements where applicable;
- a short technical explanation and a short business explanation;
- the operational implication: monitoring, prevention, recovery, security or cost as relevant;
- if an agent was used, the work contract, permissions, independent verification, challenge attempted and human decision.

## Definition of done

1. Write the requirements and non-requirements separately. 2. Identify at least two plausible architectures. 3. Quantify the important workload/SLO assumptions. 4. Record rejected alternatives and why they were rejected. 5. Define the first production measurements that would validate the design. 6. State the conditions under which you would stop the rollout.

## If your answer differs

That is acceptable when your evidence supports it and your solution respects the ticket constraints. Compare the **reasoning and evidence**, not just the final code or wording.
