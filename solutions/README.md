# Solutions & Reference Answers

These are **reference solutions**, not the learning path.

### Use them this way
1. Do the notebook exercise or Acme mission **without opening the solution**.
2. Run your tests and write your evidence first.
3. Open the matching reference answer.
4. Compare your **reasoning, evidence and trade-offs**, not just your final output.
5. If your answer differs, that is fine when you can defend it with evidence and the scenario constraints.

The solutions are intentionally separate from learner material so the challenge remains intact.

- `notebooks/` — reference approaches for notebooks 00–54.
- `scenarios/` — reference diagnoses and definition-of-done checks for Acme S01–S33.

**Rule:** a passing test is evidence, not the whole answer. Your explanation and production judgment still matter.
